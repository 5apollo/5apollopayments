Four ways to take a payment

Countertop terminals

The workhorse. A compact unit by the register that does one job extremely well. Tap, chip, swipe and contactless, built-in receipt printer, cash discount ready out of the box.

Retail · services · professional offices

Wireless terminals

Battery powered with cellular and WiFi, so payment happens where the customer is standing. Pay at the table, truck or job site, with cellular backup when WiFi drops.

Mobile · delivery · trades · events

Frequently asked questions

Which type of terminal do I need?

It depends on where the transaction happens and how complicated the ticket is. Counter transactions with no inventory or table management usually need only a countertop terminal. Tell us how orders move through your business and we will recommend the exact setup, including when the cheapest option is the right one.

Can I use my existing equipment?

Sometimes some equipment can be reprogrammed, but in most cases you will need new hardware. Many businesses qualify for free equipment or discounted pricing.

Are terminals cash discount ready?

Yes. Countertop terminals are cash discount ready out of the box, and we configure signage and receipt language for New York before you go live.

Do you work with Clover?

Yes. We work with Clover, Dejavoo, Valor and other major manufacturers so hardware is matched to the business rather than to one brand.

How fast can I be live?

Usually under a week. Underwriting takes a day or two, and equipment is fulfilled within 24 to 48 hours of approval.
