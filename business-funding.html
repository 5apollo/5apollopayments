Who it is available to

Funding is available once you are an active 5 Apollo processing client. It is a benefit of working with us, not a way in. If you are not yet processing with us, the starting point is a free statement analysis.

How it works, and who decides

We can connect active processing clients with a funding partner who offers merchant cash advance and alternative funding. We make the introduction and you decide whether it is right for your business. We do not underwrite it and we do not set the terms.

Important: Funding is provided by a separate, independent underwriter. 5 Apollo is not a lender and does not make credit decisions. Approval, terms and timelines are determined entirely by the funding partner, and approval is not guaranteed. Merchant cash advance and alternative funding differ from a traditional loan, so read the terms carefully before you accept.

Frequently asked questions

Do you offer business funding or loans?

We can connect active processing clients with a funding partner who offers merchant cash advance and alternative funding for things like equipment, staffing or expansion. We are not the lender.

Who sets the terms and approves the funding?

The funding partner does, entirely. 5 Apollo does not underwrite, make credit decisions, or set terms, and approval is not guaranteed.

Do I have to be a processing client?

Yes. Funding introductions are available to active 5 Apollo processing clients. If it is something you want to explore, ask once you are set up with us and we will make the introduction.
