Cash Discount Program: Stop Paying Credit Card Processing Fees

Under a cash discount program your listed prices are the card price, and customers who pay with cash get an automatic discount at the register. Here is how it works, who it fits, and what New York requires.

Updated October 2026 · 5 Apollo · Brooklyn, NY

How a cash discount program works

Your listed prices are the credit card prices, meaning the cost of accepting cards is already built in. When a customer chooses to pay with cash, the system automatically applies a discount at the register. You protect your margins, and cash buyers get a perk.

Cash discounting is a legal program in all 50 states. It is also optional: you are free to choose traditional processing instead, and if you ever want to switch programs, we will help you do it.

What it is worth: the math

Start with your effective rate: total fees charged ÷ total card volume. As an illustration, a business paying 3.5% on $50,000 a month in card volume is losing $1,750 a month, or $21,000 a year, to processing fees. That is the figure a cash discount program is designed to attack. The calculator on our homepage runs your own numbers, and a free statement review confirms them against your real statement.

Doing it compliantly in New York

New York’s rules on credit card pricing are stricter than most of the country, and the setup details matter: which price is posted, where, and how the terminal prints it. Cash discount and dual pricing have both been treated as permitted, while a surcharge added only at checkout is not. Our guide to New York’s GBL §518 walks through the state’s own examples. Frequently asked questions

What is a cash discount program? Do we have to use it?

Cash discounting is a legal program in all 50 states. Your listed prices are the credit card prices, and customers who pay with cash get an automatic discount at the register. It typically offers the greatest savings, but you are free to choose traditional processing instead.

Will my customers be annoyed?

Some will notice. Very few walk. What determines the reaction is whether it feels like a surprise, so clear signage at the door and the counter, and staff who can explain it in one sentence, is the whole game.

Why don’t I switch to cash discount with my current processor?

Most banks and national processors either do not offer the program or are content collecting your processing fees. They rarely walk you through the reprogramming or equipment changes.

Can I use my existing equipment?

Sometimes some equipment can be reprogrammed, but in most cases you will need new hardware. Many businesses we work with qualify for free equipment or discounted pricing.

I’m locked into a contract. Can I switch?

In our experience many contracts can be cancelled for $300–$500, and the monthly savings usually cover that quickly. Bring your agreement and latest statement and we will review the exit terms together. Sometimes the right move is to wait, and we will say so.

Is a cash discount the same as a surcharge in New York?

No. A surcharge adds a fee at checkout and requires up-front disclosure of the total card price under GBL §518. A cash discount lists the card price and discounts cash. Our NY surcharge guide explains the difference.
